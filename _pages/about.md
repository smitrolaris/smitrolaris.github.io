I am a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Maryland, College Park, where I am fortunate to be advised by [Sennur Ulukus](https://user.eng.umd.edu/~ulukus/). Broadly, I am interested in the design and analysis of algorithms for scheduling and resource allocation problems in networked systems. Some of my recent work focuses on quantum networks where I study scheduling algorithms under age-based performance metrics that go beyond throughput.

Before coming to Maryland, I earned my diploma (BSc and integrated MEng) in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the Technical University of Crete, Greece, where I also had the privilege of working with [Angelos Bletsas](https://www.winlab.rutgers.edu/~aggelos/) on belief propagation algorithms for distributed resource allocation in wireless networks.
